STANDARD FREELANCE CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and between Beacon Creative Studio LLC ("Client") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Contractor").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Contractor shall perform the design and consulting services described in Statement of Work #1. Any changes or additions to the scope of work must be agreed upon in writing by both parties before Contractor begins work on such additions, and shall be billed at Contractor's standard rate.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Client agrees to pay Contractor within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice. Invoices shall be submitted bi-weekly. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. Client shall reimburse Contractor for all reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses within fifteen (15) days of receipt of documentation.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Upon receipt of full payment of all amounts due, Contractor assigns to Client all copyrights and ownership rights in the final deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement. Contractor retains full ownership of all pre-existing tools, code libraries, workflows, and methodologies, and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such pre-existing materials as incorporated in the deliverables.

4. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
Each party agrees to defend and indemnify the other party from and against any third-party claims, damages, or liabilities arising directly from the indemnifying party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or material breach of this Agreement. Neither party shall be liable to indemnify the other for claims caused by the other party's own acts or omissions.

5. MUTUAL LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages. Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees actually paid or payable by Client to Contractor under this Agreement during the six (6) months preceding the claim.

6. NON-SOLICITATION
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of six (6) months following termination, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee or contractor of the other party who was directly involved in this project. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Contractor from providing services to other clients, including competitors of Client.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days prior written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for cause if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of written notice. Upon termination, Client shall pay Contractor for all services rendered and approved through the effective date of termination.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflicts of law principles. The parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute amicably through good-faith negotiation for thirty (30) days before initiating mediation or arbitration. The prevailing party in any proceeding shall be entitled to reasonable attorney fees.
