RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (RISKY DRAFT)

SECTION 1: LANDLORD ENTRY AND PRIVACY
Landlord reserves the unrestricted right to enter the leased premises at any time of day or night without prior notice to Tenant for inspections, alterations, showings, repairs, or any other purpose.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT FORFEITURE
The security deposit of $3,000 shall be retained in full by Landlord upon lease termination as a non-refundable administrative turnover fee, regardless of premises condition, cleanliness, or tenancy duration.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY AND STRUCTURAL REPAIR WAIVER
Tenant waives all implied warranties of habitability and shall be solely responsible for all maintenance and repairs, including structural roof leaks, central heating systems, plumbing blockages, and electrical wiring at Tenant sole expense.

SECTION 4: DEFAULT AND SUMMARY EVICTION
In the event of any rent payment delay exceeding twenty-four (24) hours, Landlord may change locks immediately, remove Tenant personal belongings, and terminate tenancy without judicial proceedings, court order, or statutory notice.

SECTION 5: QUIET ENJOYMENT
Landlord covenants that Tenant, upon timely payment of rent, shall peacefully hold and enjoy the premises during the term of this lease.
