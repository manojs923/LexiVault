STANDARD RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on February 1, 2026, between Oakwood Residential Management LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned tenant ("Tenant") for the premises located at 120 Maple Street, Apartment 2A.

1. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850 due on the first day of each month. A five (5) day grace period applies before any late fee is incurred. Late fees are capped at $50 or 5% of monthly rent, whichever is less. Electronic payment, ACH, and standard checks are accepted without processing surcharges.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall provide a security deposit of $1,850 (one month's rent), which Landlord will hold in a segregated, interest-bearing escrow account. Landlord shall return the full deposit within twenty-one (21) days of lease expiration, accompanied by an itemized written statement of any deductions for damages beyond reasonable wear and tear.

3. LANDLORD ENTRY AND TENANT PRIVACY
Landlord shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice prior to entering the premises for routine maintenance, inspections, or prospective tenant showings. Entry shall occur only during reasonable business hours (8:00 AM to 6:00 PM), except in cases of bona fide emergency threatening life or property.

4. HABITABILITY AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the premises in full compliance with all applicable housing, health, and safety codes, including heating, water, plumbing, electrical, and weatherproofing. Urgent repairs affecting essential services shall be commenced within forty-eight (48) hours of written notice. Tenant is responsible only for minor maintenance and damages caused by Tenant's own negligence.

5. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION
Landlord shall defend and indemnify Tenant from claims resulting from Landlord's negligence or failure to maintain common areas. Tenant shall defend and indemnify Landlord from claims resulting from Tenant's negligence or intentional misconduct inside the leased premises. Neither party indemnifies the other for the other party's own negligent acts.

6. GUESTS AND SUBLETTING
Tenant may host casual guests for up to fourteen (14) consecutive days without prior notice. Tenant may request permission to sublet or assign the lease, and Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay consent.

7. TERMINATION AND RENEWAL
Either party may terminate or request non-renewal at the end of the term with sixty (60) days written notice. In the event of an early termination by Tenant due to military relocation or job transfer, Tenant may terminate upon thirty (30) days notice with payment of a reasonable one-month re-letting fee.
