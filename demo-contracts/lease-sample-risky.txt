RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into on February 1, 2026, between Ironclad Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned occupant ("Tenant") for the premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B.

1. RENT AND PAYMENT
Tenant shall pay rent of $2,400 per month, due on the first day of each calendar month. If rent is received after 11:59 PM on the first of the month, Tenant shall pay an immediate late charge of $350 plus $50 for each day thereafter until rent is paid in full. Landlord accepts payment exclusively via non-certified personal money order delivered by hand.

2. NON-REFUNDABLE DEPOSIT AND FEES
Tenant shall deposit $7,200 upon signing as a non-refundable preparation fee and damage deposit. Under no circumstances shall any portion of this deposit be refunded to Tenant upon move-out, regardless of apartment condition.

3. LANDLORD ENTRY WITHOUT NOTICE
Landlord, its agents, and contractors may enter the premises at any hour of the day or night without prior notice to Tenant for inspections, repairs, showings, or any other purpose deemed appropriate by Landlord. Tenant shall not change locks or install interior security devices.

4. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE DISCLAIMER
Tenant accepts the premises in "AS-IS" condition. Landlord disclaims any implied warranty of habitability. Tenant is solely responsible for all maintenance and repairs of plumbing, heating, air conditioning, and electrical systems, regardless of the cause of failure or pre-existing conditions.

5. UNILATERAL TENANT INDEMNITY
Tenant shall fully indemnify, hold harmless, and defend Landlord from any and all damages, slip-and-fall injuries, mold exposure, water leaks, or criminal acts occurring on the premises, even if caused solely by Landlord's failure to maintain common structural components or gross negligence.

6. GUESTS AND SUBLETTING
No guests are permitted on the premises for longer than four (4) consecutive hours without Landlord's written consent and payment of a $100 per guest per day fee. Subletting, roommate additions, or temporary occupancy by family members is strictly prohibited under penalty of immediate eviction.

7. TERMINATION AND FORFEITURE
If Tenant breaches any rule or noise guideline, Landlord may declare the lease terminated immediately, lock out Tenant, and confiscate all personal property left in the unit as liquidated damages. Tenant remains liable for rent for the entire remaining term of the lease.
