RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (FAIR STANDARD DRAFT)

SECTION 1: LANDLORD ENTRY
Landlord may enter the premises only upon providing at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, and solely during normal business hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM), except in cases of immediate emergency.

SECTION 2: SECURITY DEPOSIT
Landlord shall hold the security deposit of $1,500 in an escrow account. Deposit shall be returned to Tenant within twenty-one (21) days of vacancy, less any itemized deductions for damages beyond reasonable wear and tear.

SECTION 3: HABITABILITY AND ESSENTIAL SERVICES
Landlord warrants that the premises are fit for human habitation and agrees to maintain all plumbing, heating, electrical, and structural components in good working order at Landlord expense.

SECTION 4: DEFAULT AND NOTICE TO CURE
In the event of non-payment of rent, Landlord shall provide a five (5) day written notice to cure before initiating any formal legal proceedings. No lockouts or self-help evictions are permitted.

SECTION 5: PEACEFUL POSSESSION
Tenant paying the rent and performing lease covenants shall peacefully enjoy the premises without interruption by Landlord.
